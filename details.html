Oshawott

#501 — Sea Otter Pokémon

Character Details

Name: Oshawott

Pokédex Number: #501

Universe: Pokémon

Region: Unova

Type: Water

Species: Sea Otter Pokémon

Ability: Torrent

Backstory

Oshawott is a Water-type starter Pokémon originally introduced in the Unova region. It carries a shell called a scalchop on its stomach. The scalchop can be removed and used during battles as a weapon.

Abilities

Oshawott specializes in Water-type attacks and can learn moves such as Water Gun, Aqua Jet, and Razor Shell. Its scalchop allows it to defend itself and perform powerful attacks.

Personality

Oshawott is often portrayed as energetic, confident, playful, and determined. It can also be competitive and enjoys proving itself during battles.

Evolution

Oshawott evolves into Dewott, which can later evolve into Samurott.
